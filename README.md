##  Project Overview

This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) data to understand price-index movements across different consumption categories, years, months, and geographical sectors.

The project uses Microsoft Excel to perform year-wise analysis, monthly comparisons, category-level analysis, percentage-change calculations, and correlation analysis.

The primary focus is on understanding changes in the General Index, comparing Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors, evaluating selected consumption categories before and after COVID-19, and examining the relationship between Fuel and Light and other categories.

The workbook contains nine analytical worksheets, from **Sheet 4 to Sheet 12**, each designed to investigate a different aspect of CPI data.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze year-wise CPI General Index trends.
* Compare CPI index values across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors.
* Understand the distribution of CPI observations across broader consumption categories.
* Examine monthly changes in Meat and Fish, Milk and Products, Fruits, and Vegetables.
* Compare food-category CPI index values between 2022 and 2023.
* Investigate pre- and post-COVID trends in Food and Beverages, Health, and Household Goods and Services.
* Compare Fuel and Light with Cereals and Products, Clothing and Footwear, Non-alcoholic Beverages, and Transport and Communication.
* Calculate percentage changes in selected CPI index series.
* Explore correlations between changes in Fuel and Light and selected consumption categories.
* Generate meaningful insights from historical CPI data.

##  Key Analytical Findings

Based on the displayed summaries in the workbook, the following observations can be highlighted:

1. **Long-term index growth:** The annual average General Index increases across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors from 2016 to 2023.

2. **Sector differences:** Rural index values are consistently higher than Urban index values in the year-wise General Index summary.

3. **Food category distribution:** Food represents approximately 44.44% of the classified observations in the broader-category summary.

4. **Food-category variation:** Spices, Cereals and Products, and Milk and Products show increases in their displayed 2023 annual average index values compared with 2022, while Oils and Fats and Vegetables show decreases.

5. **Category-specific trends:** Health has a higher displayed annual average index value in 2023 than Food and Beverages and Household Goods and Services.

6. **Monthly fluctuations:** The selected food categories exhibit different monthly patterns, demonstrating why individual categories should be analyzed separately.

7. **Fuel comparisons:** The final four worksheets provide a framework for investigating relationships between Fuel and Light and selected food, clothing, beverage, and transportation categories.

These findings describe the displayed CPI index data. They should not be interpreted as proof of the causes of inflation or as official inflation-rate estimates.

## 📐 Calculations Used

### 1. Percentage Change

The percentage change between two index values is calculated as:

Percentage Change = ((Current Index − Previous Index) / Previous Index) × 100

In Excel:

```excel
=(Current_Value-Previous_Value)/Previous_Value
```

Format the result as a percentage when appropriate.

### 2. Correlation

Correlation can be used to measure the direction and strength of the linear relationship between two numerical series.

In Excel:

```excel
=CORREL(range1,range2)
```

A positive correlation indicates that the two series tend to move in the same direction, while a negative correlation indicates that they tend to move in opposite directions.

Correlation does not establish causation.
